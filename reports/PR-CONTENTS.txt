Start with shared-compiler.txt for the scope and validation of the current PR.

The llama-* subdirectories retain historical investigation evidence. Those
measurements used earlier experimental checkpoints and presets that have
since been removed. They do not establish current default-path throughput.

The complete 3.4 GiB local experiment archive is not part of the PR. Historical
findings refer to local artifacts that may not be published here. Model files,
executables, raw .f32 reference/candidate vectors, generated shader dumps, and
rejected native-backend prototypes are excluded. Source and compact validation
results are retained; the excluded experiments are not build dependencies.

The correctness tool now compares saved vector directories with explicit
reference/candidate phase logs. It loads no model. Historical capture recipes
refer to removed experimental hooks and are not current reproduction commands.
